### Dorothy Stratten Case — Complete Information

#### **Basic Overview**
Dorothy Ruth Hoogstraten (February 28, 1960 – August 14, 1980) was a Canadian model and actress, best known as Playboy's Playmate of the Year for 1980. She was murdered by her estranged husband Paul Snider at age 20 in a murder-suicide.

#### **Life & Career**
- Born in Vancouver, British Columbia to Dutch-immigrant parents
- Discovered by Paul Snider (age 26, club promoter and pimp) while working at a Dairy Queen in 1977 as a teenager
- Snider forged her mother's signature on model release forms and sent her photos to Playboy in summer 1978
- Moved to Los Angeles in August 1978; married Snider in June 1979
- Became Playboy's Miss August 1979 and Playmate of the Year in 1980 (presented April 30, 1980)
- Awarded $200,000 in cash and gifts with the Playmate of the Year title
- Worked as a Playboy Bunny at the Playboy Club in Century City, Los Angeles
- Had small TV and film roles: *Buck Rogers*, *Fantasy Island*, *Americathon*, *Skatetown U.S.A.*, *Autumn Born*, and *Galaxina*

#### **The Relationship with Bogdanovich**
- March 1980: Began filming *They All Laughed* with director Peter Bogdanovich in New York
- She and Bogdanovich began an affair immediately upon her arrival (consummated the day after she arrived)
- In late June 1980, she wrote to Snider announcing they were separated
- July 30, 1980: She and Bogdanovich returned to Los Angeles after a 10-day holiday in England
- She moved into Bogdanovich's mansion in Bel Air, though officially her residence was listed as a Beverly Hills apartment

#### **Snider's Escalation**
- Became increasingly obsessed and violent as he realized Stratten was having an affair with Bogdanovich
- July 31, 1980: Hid outside Bogdanovich's mansion carrying a borrowed handgun, intending to shoot anyone at the entrance; left after several hours
- Emptied their joint bank account
- Hired a private detective to gather evidence of infidelity
- Sold Stratten's Playmate prizes (including a Jaguar worth $26,000) for cash

#### **Gun Purchase Timeline**
- August 8, 1980: Snider met with Stratten; she told him she'd fallen in love with Bogdanovich and wanted to finalize their separation
- August 9: Attempted to buy a gun at a local store but was refused due to his Canadian citizenship
- August 9-10: Tried to get the private detective to buy a gun for him; detective refused
- August 11: Tried to find a gun in the newspaper classifieds but got lost
- **August 13**: Successfully purchased a used 12-gauge pump-action shotgun from a private seller; told friends he was "going to take up hunting"

#### **The Murder**
- **August 14, 1980, noon**: Stratten arrived at Snider's West Los Angeles house for a meeting to discuss their separation settlement
- She brought $1,100 in cash as a down payment on the property settlement
- Snider's two roommates left in the morning; the couple was alone
- **Late afternoon (within an hour of her arrival)**: Snider shot Stratten in his bedroom with the shotgun
- **Approximately one hour later**: Snider shot himself with the same shotgun
- **After 11:00 PM, August 14**: The roommates and a private detective (who'd called expressing concern) discovered both bodies in the bedroom—both nude, each killed by a single shotgun blast
- **August 15, after midnight**: The private detective called Hugh Hefner at the Playboy Mansion to inform him; Hefner called Bogdanovich
- Stratten's body was cremated and buried at Westwood Village Memorial Park Cemetery in Los Angeles

#### **Disputed Allegations: Hefner & Stratten**
Peter Bogdanovich's 1984 book *The Killing of the Unicorn* alleged that Hugh Hefner sexually assaulted Stratten in August 1978 (when she was 18) in a secluded area of the Playboy Mansion. Key developments:
- Bogdanovich initially wrote "raped" but changed it to "seduced" after threats of a lawsuit
- Hefner denied the assault, claiming it was consensual "hugging" in the Jacuzzi and that his sexual interest ended after learning she expected to become engaged
- In 2022, Stefan Tetenbaum (Hefner's valet 1978-1981) alleged on the A&E documentary *Secrets of Playboy* that he and a security guard witnessed Hefner raping Stratten in the mansion grotto

#### **Legacy & Aftermath**

**Films about her:**
- *Death of a Centerfold: The Dorothy Stratten Story* (1981 TV movie) — Jamie Lee Curtis as Stratten
- *Star 80* (1983 Bob Fosse film) — Mariel Hemingway as Stratten; Eric Roberts as Snider
- *Welcome to Chippendales* (2022 Hulu miniseries) — Nicola Peltz as Stratten

**Her final film:**
- *They All Laughed* (1981) — Released posthumously; initially flopped
- Bogdanovich spent his entire net worth ($5+ million) trying to re-release and promote it; filed for bankruptcy in 1985 and lost his home

**Books:**
- *The Killing of the Unicorn: Dorothy Stratten 1960–1980* (1984) — Bogdanovich's controversial memoir/biography

**Songs dedicated to her:**
- Bryan Adams: "The Best Was Yet to Come" (1983)
- Bryan Adams & Lindsay Mitchell: "Cover Girl" (1980)
- Bush: "Dead Meat"
- Red Hot Chili Peppers: reference in "Californication"

**Personal aftermath:**
- In 1988, Bogdanovich married Stratten's sister Louise (then 20; he was 49). He'd paid for her schooling and modeling classes after Dorothy's death. They divorced in 2001.
- Bogdanovich was buried next to Stratten at Westwood Village Memorial Park Cemetery following his death in 2022
- Her epitaph includes a passage from Ernest Hemingway's *A Farewell to Arms*, chosen by Bogdanovich
