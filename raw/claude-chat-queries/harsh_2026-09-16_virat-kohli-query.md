## Claude Response 1
Virat Kohli is an Indian professional cricketer and former captain of the Indian national team. He's widely regarded as one of the greatest batsmen in cricket history.

Key facts:

- Born in 1988, Delhi-based.
- Captained India's Test and ODI teams from 2014-2021 (stepping down from ODI captaincy in 2021, Test captaincy in 2022).
- Holds multiple records: fastest to 8,000, 9,000, 10,000, and 11,000 ODI runs; most centuries in ODI cricket (46); among the highest run-scorers across all three cricket formats (Test, ODI, T20).
- Has captained India in World Cups and won IPL (Indian Premier League) titles with Royal Challengers Bangalore.
- Known for aggressive batting style, fitness, and consistency across all three cricket formats (Test, ODI, T20).
- One of the highest-paid athletes in the world; has endorsement deals with major brands (Nike, MRF, others).
- Currently plays international cricket and IPL; has had periods of reduced form but remains a key player for India.
