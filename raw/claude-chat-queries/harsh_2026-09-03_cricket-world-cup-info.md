## Comprehensive Information on Cricket World Cup

The Cricket World Cup is an international cricket championship held every four years, considered the premier contest in the one-day international (ODI) format, and one of the most-watched sporting events globally. It is organized by the International Cricket Council (ICC).

### History & Origins

The 1975 Cricket World Cup (officially called the Prudential Cup '75) was the inaugural men's World Cup, and the first major tournament in the history of One Day International cricket. It took place in England, between 7 June and 21 June 1975. The first Women's Cricket World Cup was held in 1973, while the first Men's Cricket World Cup was held in 1975. Both tournaments were played in England and comprised one-day international matches of 60 overs per side.

### Format Evolution

The inaugural tournament had eight teams split into two groups of four, with each match played over 60 overs per side. The tournament was played outside of England for the first time in 1987, when India and Pakistan hosted. The overs dropped to 50 per team for these games. In 1992, the tournament introduced coloured clothing, white balls, day and night games, and fielding restrictions.

### Men's World Cup Winners

As of 2025 the Australian men's cricket team has won six times (1987, 1999, 2003, 2007, 2015, 2023). Other notable winners include:
- West Indies won the first edition in 1975 and again in 1979
- India (1983, 2011)
- Pakistan (1992)
- Sri Lanka (1996)

### Women's World Cup Winners

The Australian women's cricket team has seven wins (1978, 1982, 1988, 1997, 2005, 2013, 2022). They are also the only teams to have won three consecutive tournaments, with the women's team winning three straight titles in 1978, 1982, and 1988.

### Recent Tournaments

**2023 ODI World Cup (Men's):**
The 2023 Cricket World Cup Final was played at the Narendra Modi Stadium in Ahmedabad, on 19 November 2023. Australia defeated India to win a record-extending sixth World Cup title. Australia beat India in the final by six wickets. Travis Head (137 off 120) and Marnus Labuschagne (58 not out in 110) guided Australia to victory in 43 overs, while India was bowled out on 240. Virat Kohli ended as the top-scorer at the 2023 ODI World Cup with 765 runs while Mohammed Shami, with 24 wickets, was the top wicket-taker.

**2025 Women's World Cup:**
The 2025 ICC Women's Cricket World Cup final was played at the DY Patil Stadium in Navi Mumbai on 2 November 2025. India won by 52 runs with Shafali Verma named Player of the Match.

**2026 Men's T20 World Cup:**
The 2026 ICC Men's T20 World Cup final was played at the Narendra Modi Stadium in Ahmedabad on 8 March 2026. India won by 96 runs against New Zealand, with Jasprit Bumrah named Player of the Match.
