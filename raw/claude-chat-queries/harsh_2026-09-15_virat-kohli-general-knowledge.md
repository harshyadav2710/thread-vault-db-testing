Virat Kohli is an Indian cricketer and former captain of the Indian national cricket team. He's widely regarded as one of the greatest batsmen of modern cricket.

Key facts:
- Born November 5, 1988; Delhi-based
- Captained India's Test and ODI teams (2014-2021)
- Plays for Royal Challengers Bangalore in the Indian Premier League
- Known for aggressive batting style, fitness obsession, and competitive intensity
- Multiple records: highest ODI run-scorer at retirement (2023), multiple Test centuries, consistent performer across formats
- Career highlights include leading India to World Cup semi-final (2015), Test series wins, and individual milestones
- Stepped down as Test captain in 2021; remains central figure in Indian cricket but no longer skipper
- Personal brand extends beyond cricket: fitness influencer, endorsements, significant social media presence
- Married to Bollywood actress Anushka Sharma; two children
