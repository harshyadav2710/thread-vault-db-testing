## Response

Based on search of emails and CRM data, I identified **3GM Steel** as the primary AskCruz prospect.

### **Prospect Overview**
3GM Steel is the main prospect for AskCruz deployment, with a formal proposal issued on August 13, 2026. The company received a six-week implementation plan beginning immediately after contract signature.

### **Requirements & Pain Points**

**Operational Challenges:**
- **Data silos**: Email, ERP (EOXS), and operational data scattered across multiple systems with no unified query interface
- **Team scalability**: 15 users (including salespeople and 4 partners) need consistent access to customer, order, and historical information
- **Manual information retrieval**: Currently time-consuming to find answers on customers, orders, and conversation history across platforms
- **Lack of central visibility**: No single source of truth for operational decisions

### **Why AskCruz is Relevant to 3GM**

**Direct Solution Match:**

1. **Company Brain unification** — AskCruz creates a unified knowledge base from 3GM's email (all 15 mailboxes), EOXS ERP integration, and operational data—giving the entire team instant answers on customers, orders, pricing, and history without manual searching.

2. **Scale to all users** — Rather than limiting AI tools to technical staff, AskCruz makes the full power available to salespeople and partners with simple natural-language queries.

3. **Real-time data sync** — Daily automatic email ingestion and live EOXS connection mean answers are always current—critical for a trading/distribution company where pricing and inventory change constantly.

4. **Management visibility** — Prompt History logging gives leadership insight into what queries the team is solving with AI, surfacing new workflow automation opportunities.

5. **Workflow automation** — Built-in AI agent capability to automate recurring tasks (e.g., order confirmations, customer summaries, pricing lookups) as operational needs emerge.

**Business Context:**
3GM Steel is a mid-market steel distribution operation with complex multi-user operations and partner involvement. They currently lack an AI layer to reason over their distributed operational data—exactly the gap AskCruz fills. The proposal indicates executive buy-in from Travis Lane (likely leadership) and Stefan Brown (another key stakeholder), with immediate go-live readiness across 15 users.
