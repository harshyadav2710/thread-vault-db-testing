## KEY FINDINGS ON EOXS PRODUCT

**Product Name:** Velox (Odoo-based ERP)

**Core Capabilities:**
- Inventory management with multi-warehouse support
- Sales order and purchasing order management
- Gross profit calculation and cost tracking
- Customizable workflows and approval processes
- Freight and landed cost management
- Financial reporting and dashboards
- Cash waterfall/liquidity forecasting
- CRM integration for pipeline management

**Target Market:** Steel and metal distribution, manufacturing, and service centers with complex operations

**Typical Customer Profile:**
- 15-100+ employees
- Annual revenue $0.5M-$5M+
- Manual or legacy systems currently in use
- Multi-location operations
- Complex inventory and costing needs
- Established player in their market

**Key Differentiators:**
- Specialized customization for steel/metal industry
- Pragmatic approach to implementation (not forcing generic solutions)
- Support for division-level implementations at enterprise scale
