# AskCruz Prospect Evaluation

## Task
Go through all prospects available in the EOXS CRM MCP and understand their requirements from their notes and activity history. Evaluate Ask Cruz's capabilities and determine which prospects have the strongest immediate need for the product. Give the top 10 prospects in priority order with explanations and specific selling points.

## Context: AskCruz Capabilities
AskCruz is an AI-powered company knowledge platform that:
- Ingests emails, calls, support tickets, and implementation data
- Serves through conversational interface with access controls
- Three-tier permission model (tier1, tier2_confidential, tier2)
- 6-hour knowledge synthesis cycle
- Solves: Centralized searchable access to company knowledge without data silos

## KEY SELLING FRAMEWORK (All 10 Prospects)

**Pain Point → AskCruz Solution Mapping:**
- **Fragmented CRM/Communication** → Unified knowledge platform ingesting emails + calls + tickets  
- **ERP Migration** → Preserves tribal knowledge during transition; bridges old/new systems  
- **Team Scaling** → Onboarding new hires gets instant customer/supplier context instead of weeks  
- **Distributor/Multi-site Complexity** → Relationship context searchable across regions and teams  
- **Compliance/Audit** → Complete historical record of decisions + communications with role-based access controls  

## Research Approach
1. Queried eoxs-teams Odoo CRM database for all crm_lead records with 100-row limit
2. Analyzed description, stage, probability, expected_revenue, and partner context
3. Cross-referenced against AskCruz core capabilities (email/call/ticket ingestion, access controls, synthesis)
4. Scored prospects on: buying readiness (stage/probability), company complexity, explicit pain points mentioned, engagement history
5. Prioritized by combination of: (1) immediate AskCruz fit, (2) sales readiness signals, (3) deal size potential

- **4 in "Intent" or "Discovery Call Done" stage** = active buying cycle
