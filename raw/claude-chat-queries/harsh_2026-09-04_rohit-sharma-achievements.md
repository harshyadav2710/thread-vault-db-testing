## Claude Response

Here are Rohit Sharma's major achievements as a cricketer:

### International Trophies & Captain Achievements
Rohit led the Indian side to victory in the 2024 T20 World Cup, and under his leadership, India were crowned champions in the ICC Men's Champions Trophy in 2025, winning both tournaments unbeaten. He also led India to the final of the ICC Men's Cricket World Cup 2023 and WTC 2023.

### Batting Records & Milestones
Rohit holds the record for scoring three ODI double-centuries and the highest individual ODI score of all time at 264. Across the three international formats he has scored more than 20,000 runs, with 4,301 runs in 67 Test matches with 12 hundreds, above 11,500 ODI runs, and 4,231 T20I runs. In T20Is, Rohit is the highest run-getter of all time and scored 19,700 runs across 499 international matches.

### National & International Awards
Rohit received the Arjuna Award in 2015, the Rajiv Gandhi Khel Ratna Award (now Major Dhyan Chand Khel Ratna Award) in 2020, and the Padma Shri in January 2026. He was named the International Cricket Council Men's One Day International Cricketer of the Year in 2019.

### Career Format Achievements
He announced his retirement from T20I cricket on June 29, 2024, after leading India to victory in the T20I World Cup, and retired from Test cricket on May 7, 2025. Rohit made his ODI and T20I debuts in 2007 and won the inaugural T20 World Cup with India that same year, while his Test debut came in November 2013.
