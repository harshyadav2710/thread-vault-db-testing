# Virat Kohli Information

## User Query

## Assistant Response
Virat Kohli (note the spelling) is an Indian professional cricketer and one of the most prominent athletes in the world. Key facts:

- **Role**: Right-handed batsman and occasional off-spin bowler
- **Captaincy**: Former captain of the Indian national cricket team (2014–2021 in Test cricket, stepped down from ODI and T20I captaincy in 2022)
- **Era**: Emerged as a superstar in the 2010s and remains at elite level through the 2020s
- **Playing style**: Known for aggressive batting, consistency, and fitness; often compared to Sachin Tendulkar
- **Career highlights**: Multiple centuries across all formats, holds several international cricket records, plays in the IPL (Indian Premier League) for Royal Challengers Bangalore
- **Global presence**: One of the most followed athletes on social media globally

He's considered one of the greatest batsmen of his generation and is central to modern Indian cricket.
