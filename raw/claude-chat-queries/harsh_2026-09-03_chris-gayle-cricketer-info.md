### Basic Profile
- Full Name: Christopher Henry Gayle
- Born: September 21, 1979 in Kingston, Jamaica
- Age: 46 years old
- Height: 6 ft 3 in (191 cm)
- Batting: Left-handed
- Bowling: Right-arm off-break
- Role: All-rounder

### International Cricket Career
- Represented West Indies: 1999-2021
- Test debut: March 16, 2000 vs Zimbabwe
- ODI debut: September 11, 1999 vs India
- T20I debut: February 16, 2006 vs New Zealand
- Captained West Indies: 2007-2010
- Last Test: September 5, 2014 vs Bangladesh
- Last ODI: August 14, 2019 vs India
- Last T20I: November 6, 2021 vs Australia

### Test Career Statistics
- 103 Test matches
- 7,214 runs
- Average: 42
- 15 centuries (including 2 triple hundreds)
- 1,046 fours
- 98 sixes

### ODI Career Statistics
- 301 ODI matches
- 10,480 runs
- Average: 38
- 25 centuries
- Highest ODI score: 215 vs Zimbabwe (2015 World Cup) - first West Indian double hundred in ODI cricket

### T20I Career
- 2 T20I centuries
- Regarded as greatest Twenty20 batsman in cricket history

### IPL Career
- 142 matches
- 4,965 runs
- 6 centuries
- 31 half-centuries
- Highest IPL score: 175* (Royal Challengers Bangalore, 2013) - remains highest individual score in T20 cricket
- IPL Teams: Kolkata Knight Riders (2009-2010), Royal Challengers Bangalore (2011-2017), Punjab Kings (2018-2021)

### Major International Achievements
- 2004 ICC Champions Trophy winner
- 2012 ICC World Twenty20 winner
- 2016 ICC World Twenty20 winner

### Playing Style & Legacy
- Described as big-hitting Jamaican left-hander
- Known for audacity and career built on attacking cricket
- Became synonymous with T20 batting
- Had rare ability to turn good-length deliveries into boundary balls
- Even after international retirement, remained iconic in T20 leagues

### Other Teams Played For
- Domestic: Jamaica, Western Australia
- League: Somerset, Sydney Thunder, Fortune Barishal, Jamaica Tallawahs, Jozi Stars, Rangpur Riders, St Kitts and Nevis Patriots
