# MS Dhoni Question

## User Message
who is ms dhoni

## Assistant Response

MS Dhoni is a legendary Indian cricket player and former captain of the Indian national cricket team.

**Career Highlights:**
- Wicket-keeper batsman known for his calm demeanor and finishing ability
- Led India to win the ICC Cricket World Cup in 2011 (ODI) and ICC World T20 in 2007
- Played 90 Test matches, 350 ODIs, and 98 T20Is for India
- Retired from international cricket in August 2020

**Playing Style:**
- Famous for his "helicopter shot" and aggressive batting in high-pressure moments
- Known as "Captain Cool" for his composure under pressure
- Exceptional wicket-keeper with quick reflexes

**Post-Cricket:**
- Currently plays for Chennai Super Kings in the Indian Premier League (IPL)
- One of the most successful IPL captains
- Widely regarded as one of cricket's greatest finishers and leaders

He's widely considered one of the greatest cricketers and captains in Indian cricket history, credited with transforming India's fortunes in limited-overs cricket.
