# NLP Definition Query

**User:** what do you mean by NLP

**Claude:** 

**NLP** stands for **Natural Language Processing** — the field of AI that focuses on how computers understand, interpret, and generate human language.

Common NLP tasks include:
- **Sentiment analysis** — detecting emotion/opinion in text
- **Text classification** — categorizing documents or messages
- **Named entity recognition** — identifying people, places, companies, dates in text
- **Machine translation** — translating between languages
- **Question answering** — finding answers to questions posed in natural language
- **Information extraction** — pulling structured data from unstructured text
- **Summarization** — condensing long documents into key points
- **Speech recognition** — converting spoken words to text

In the context of your work — AskCruz, the knowledge LLM system, and internal tools — NLP is the underlying tech stack that powers understanding and retrieval of company information, parsing customer queries, and generating contextual responses.
