# Brannon Steel Lookup

## User Query
User asked for details on Brannon Steel.

## Response

Brannon Steel is a North American manufacturer of carbon steel parts, family-owned since 1968. Based in Brampton, Ontario.

Key facts:
- $26.7M annual revenue, 98 employees
- Grew organically from 20 to 170 employees (older figure), currently occupying three buildings in Brampton
- Services construction, defense, energy, forestry, and mining sectors
- Serves major OEMs like Caterpillar
- ISO 9001 and ISO 14001 certified, uses Lean Manufacturing, Six Sigma, and offers KanBan/Kitting/JIT programs
- Received a Silver Award from Caterpillar's vendor recognition program
- Processes: plasma cutting, laser cutting, saw cutting, brake forming, plate rolling, structural rolling

Known for long-tenure workforce and quality focus.
