## **What AskCruz Is**

AskCruz is an AI-powered company knowledge base and conversational assistant—essentially a "second brain" for organizations. It turns scattered business information (emails, calls, documents, operational systems) into searchable, synthesized, cited knowledge that employees can access through a single conversational interface.

**Core definition:** An industry-agnostic AI agent that connects a company's scattered business data, structures it into usable organizational knowledge, and enables employees to access that knowledge conversationally.

## **How AskCruz Works**

The system operates through a documented 10-stage pipeline:
- Source Systems → Ingestion → Filtering → Sensitivity Classification → Central Knowledge Store → Knowledge Generation → Citation Check → Human Approval → Retrieval → Response

**Data sources ingested:**
- Gmail (three individual accounts)
- Zoho (shared support inbox)
- Fireflies (business call transcripts)
- Fathom (call recordings)
- Odoo implementation/onboarding boards for configured clients

## **Capabilities**

AskCruz provides access to:
- **Email** information across Gmail/Zoho
- **Call & Meeting Data** via Fireflies/Fathom
- **Client/Implementation Data** through Odoo boards
- **Employee Information** from HR sources
- **Internal Knowledge & Documents** (policies, procedures, references)
- **Cross-Source Business Context** by combining multiple sources
