I know MS Dhoni as one of cricket's most successful and influential players:

**Career Basics**
- Former captain of the Indian national cricket team (2007–2016, also led in ODIs/Tests)
- Played primarily as wicketkeeper-batsman for India and Chennai Super Kings (IPL)
- Career span roughly 2004–2019 (international), still plays IPL

**Major Achievements**
- Won the 2011 Cricket World Cup as captain (famous last-ball six in the final against Sri Lanka)
- Led India to #1 ranking in Test cricket
- Won multiple IPL titles with Chennai Super Kings
- One of India's most decorated captains

**Playing Style**
- Known for calm, composed leadership and tactical acumen
- Excellent finisher in limited-overs cricket
- Strong wicketkeeping skills
- Built reputation for staying composed under pressure

**Legacy**
- Considered one of the greatest captains in cricket history
- Transformed Indian cricket's T20 approach
- Cultural icon in India with massive following
- Transitioned well post-retirement into business and media

**Current Status**
- Retired from international cricket in 2019
- Still plays in IPL (Chennai Super Kings)
- Involved in business ventures and endorsements
